# 📊 Multi-Touch Marketing Attribution & ROI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer journeys across multiple digital marketing channels to determine the true impact of marketing campaigns on customer conversions.

Instead of relying only on Last-Touch Attribution, this project compares multiple attribution models to fairly distribute conversion credit across all customer touchpoints. The final solution helps marketing teams optimize advertising budgets by identifying the channels that generate the highest return on investment.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Modern customers interact with several marketing channels before making a purchase.

Example Customer Journey:

Facebook Ad → Blog Article → Google Search → Purchase

Traditional analytics often assign 100% of the credit to the final interaction, ignoring earlier marketing efforts.

This project builds an attribution engine that evaluates the contribution of every touchpoint.

---
