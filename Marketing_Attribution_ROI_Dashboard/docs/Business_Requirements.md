# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

# Project Title

Multi-Touch Marketing Attribution & ROI Dashboard

---

# 1. Business Problem

Marketing teams invest in multiple advertising channels such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok, Email Marketing, and Organic Search.

Customers usually interact with multiple channels before making a purchase.

Traditional analytics platforms often use Last-Touch Attribution, which assigns 100% conversion credit to the final touchpoint.

This can lead to incorrect marketing decisions and inefficient budget allocation.

---

# 2. Business Goal

Develop an analytics solution that accurately attributes conversions across multiple customer touchpoints.

The solution should help marketing teams identify high-performing channels and improve marketing ROI.

---

# 3. Objectives

- Clean and prepare marketing datasets
- Build complete customer journeys
- Implement multiple attribution models
- Calculate marketing KPIs
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate business recommendations

---

# 4. Stakeholders

Primary Stakeholders

- Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- Marketing Manager
- Performance Marketing Team
- Business Analyst

---

# 5. Business Questions

The dashboard should answer the following questions:

- Which marketing channel generates the highest revenue?
- Which channel has the highest ROAS?
- Which campaigns have the highest CAC?
- How do attribution models affect channel performance?
- Which channels should receive more marketing budget?

---

# 6. Expected Deliverables

- Clean Marketing Dataset
- SQL Database
- Attribution Engine
- KPI Calculations
- Power BI Dashboard
- Executive Summary Report

---

# 7. Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Compare multiple attribution models
- Calculate marketing KPIs accurately
- Help identify the most profitable marketing channels
- Provide actionable business insights
